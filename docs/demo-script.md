# Suhba · three-minute walkthrough

Use a presenter-owned account. Do not expose private household information, test identities or invitation codes. Demonstrate actual saved records and distinguish sample challenges from real opportunities.

**0:00–0:25 — The purpose.** Open About Suhba. “Moving to a city means making many connected decisions. Suhba brings lived experiences and official sources together, then helps newcomers turn them into their own next steps.” Show the independent-project statement briefly.

**0:25–1:05 — Discover and understand.** Return to Discover. Choose a task or topic and open a real creator source. Show attribution, editorial summary, known publication date versus last check, uncertainty and commercial disclosure where present. Open the original publisher through the real link. Explain that tourism advice and legal guidance have different authority; personal experiences do not establish eligibility.

**1:05–1:45 — Move from information to action.** Start a goal from that source. Add your intended outcome, one unresolved question and a next step. Save it, reload and reopen the goal to demonstrate durable persistence. Show an editable inquiry draft or a Small Ask draft with only the context the user selected. Helpers are opt-in; do not claim an unrecruited live network.

**1:45–2:15 — Before you say yes.** Open a presenter-created scenario with explicitly illustrative, user-entered figures. Show cash/time totals, an unknown input and a constraint. Make a what-if copy and edit one assumption. The arithmetic is deterministic; no local price or guaranteed timing is invented.

**2:15–2:40 — A first opportunity.** Open Work samples. Explicitly identify the bounded sample challenge. Show an actual artifact, requirement mapping, gaps and review status. A work sample is evidence of contribution, not a credential or employment promise.

**2:40–3:00 — Trust and next steps.** Show private notes, controlled sharing and recoverable deletion without displaying a live invitation. “Suhba helps people discover, decide and do—with visible sources, their own assumptions and human help when they choose it.” Hosted AI is deferred and not presented as live. Close on Discover.

## Release conditions

Use the actual successful Sites deployment URL and exact published commit. Check anonymous browsing and signed-in persistence before the walkthrough. If production identity or a capability is still unverified, state that plainly and demonstrate only its tested local behavior. The event track and final submission remain the user's decision.
